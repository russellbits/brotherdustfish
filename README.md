## This is an Interactive (and expandable) Novel

This is the web site for my newest novel, "Brother Dustfish". While there is a version of the book that goes from A to Z and has a beginning middle and end (and will be available in book form soon)—*this* version is a touch more interactive. It uses a hyperarticle/hyperstory concept and HTMX to "drop in" content when the user looks for more information behind a link.

## AI

This book is 100% human written. The illustrations are AI. I would love to comission an illustrator but I am a writer and broke most of the time, so AI illustrations it is. If the book ever sells enough copies, I'll get a real illustrator.

## Editing and Suggestions

Anyone who is interested is free to contirbute to this book. Simply pull an issue for the repo and I'll consider it. The issue can take the form of an idea for the story, to any kind of spelling or grammatical edits. However, if it's clear that the suggestion is coming from AI, I mean to ignore it.

### Chapter 1
## The Desert of Idex Mortez

Able stood in the starless, crimson night, beneath the great, glowing Skybow of the Goliaths, holding a small squirming, copper rodent. It fizzled and smoked and threw out sparks in his hands. Able presses his metallic thumb to the top of the creature’s head. The lights in its eyes go out. His own eyes, circles of cyan light, scanned the creature’s wound. He lay the little desert rodent on its back and removed a bag of electrol from his backpack. He poured it liberally on the little creature's back legs and moving his hands as if he were engaged in the delicate choreography of sewing, worked the electrol into the legs where the sparkling substance formed new bonds between pistons and new shiny ligaments. After ten minutes of this, the rodent’s four back legs looked solid to Able, so he held the creature in front of him, pressed his thumb to the critter’s head, and the gold lights in its eyes returned.

"There. All fixed!"

The creature, detecting its newfound mobility, squirmed in Able's grasp and tried to get free. Able let it go where it scurried off into a dark crack between some piles of cartridges and other metallic refuse. Able stared after it. "You're welcome."
